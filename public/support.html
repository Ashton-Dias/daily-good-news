Common questions

The widget isn't showing today's story

Open the app once and pull down to refresh. The widget updates itself around 6am each day, and shows the last saved story if you are offline. If it still looks stale, remove the widget and add it again.

I'm offline. What can I read?

The app keeps the most recent stories on your device, so the Archive and your Favourites open without a connection. You will see "Offline. Showing saved stories." until it can refresh.

How do I turn the daily reminder on or off?

Tap the gear icon on the Today screen. The reminder is off by default. If you turned notifications off for the app in your iPhone's Settings, the app will explain how to allow them again.

Where do my favourites go?

Favourites are saved only on your device. They are not synced, so deleting the app removes them.

Where do the stories come from?

Each story is a short summary, written in our own words, of an article from a public good-news publisher. The source is shown on every story, and "Read the full story" opens the original.
