Daily Good News – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Short version: Daily Good News does not collect, store or share any personal information about you.

What the app does

The app and its widget download one small public file each day that contains the day's story. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

Data on your device

Stories you mark as favourites, and the most recent story (so the widget works offline), are saved only on your device. This data is never sent to us or anyone else. If you turn on the optional daily reminder, it is a local notification scheduled on your device; it contains no story text and involves no server or personal data.

Network requests

To get the daily story, the app requests a public file from our static web host (GitHub Pages). Like any web request, this exposes your IP address to that host, which may keep standard server logs under GitHub's privacy statement. We do not receive or process those logs.

Links to other sites

Each story links to its original publisher. When you tap "Read the full story", the publisher's site opens in your browser and is governed by that site's own privacy policy.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
